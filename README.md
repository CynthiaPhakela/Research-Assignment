# Business Analytics & Data Science Research Assignments

This repository contains two research assignments completed as part of the **BrightLearn Data Analytics & Business Analytics** programme. The assignments were designed to strengthen foundational knowledge in Business Analytics, Data Analytics, Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, Business Intelligence, data management, and modern analytical concepts. The work demonstrates the ability to research technical topics, interpret industry terminology, and communicate complex concepts in a clear and practical manner. Together, the assignments cover fundamental analytical concepts, data infrastructure, key performance indicators (KPIs), customer and marketing analytics, AI, APIs, cloud computing, data governance, ethics, and emerging technologies that are essential for aspiring data professionals.  

The first assignment, **Foundations of Data Analytics & Data Science**, expands on these principles by exploring databases, data warehouses, data lakes, ETL processes, Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Large Language Models (LLMs), APIs, cloud computing, Python, Git, data ethics, governance, and business intelligence tools. It also includes reflective questions that encourage learners to connect technical concepts to real-world applications and future career development. Throughout both assignments, emphasis was placed on conducting independent research, explaining concepts in original language, using practical examples, and supporting findings with credible references, reinforcing both technical understanding and academic integrity.

The second assignment, **The Language of Business Analytics**, focuses on developing a strong analytical vocabulary by researching and defining **38 essential Business Analytics concepts**. Each concept is supported by a practical business example and referenced using credible academic and industry sources. Topics include Business Analytics, Business Intelligence, Big Data, descriptive through prescriptive analytics, data visualization, dashboards, customer and sales KPIs, marketing campaign analytics, and performance measurement. The assignment emphasizes distinguishing between commonly confused concepts, applying definitions to real-world business scenarios, and understanding how data supports evidence-based decision-making.

## Referencing Style

The assignments use the **Harvard Referencing Style**, with all sources cited consistently throughout the work and a complete reference list provided at the end of the document. This aligns with the BrightLearn assignment requirements, which specify the use of a consistent citation format and credible academic and industry sources.
